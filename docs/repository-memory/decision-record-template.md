# RM-YYYY-NNN: Decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Scope: repository area and environments covered
- Owners: people or team responsible for maintaining the decision
- Supersedes: record ID or none

## Context

Describe the reviewed facts, constraints, and open questions. Link each material fact to evidence.

## Decision

State what is being decided and what is explicitly outside scope.

## Evidence

- Durable source: commit, pull request, test, report, or runbook
- External observation: system, environment, timestamp, and observer

## Verification

List completed checks separately from checks that remain blocked or pending.

## Approval

- State: not-recorded | approved | rejected
- Approver: required only for approved or rejected
- Scope: exact decision or release covered
- Evidence: durable link or record of the explicit approval

`not-recorded` is the required value unless explicit approval evidence was reviewed. Do not infer approval from merge status, organization identity, or authorship.
